A Letter For You

“

Dear Sisters,

I don't always say it. Sometimes I joke, sometimes I fight, sometimes I pretend that everything is normal. But somewhere underneath all of that, there is a very simple truth.

I am incredibly lucky to have you.

You have been part of so many chapters of my life that it is impossible to imagine the story without you in it.

I hope life gives you everything you deserve — happiness that stays, dreams that come true, people who value you, and countless reasons to smile.

And whenever life gets difficult, remember that you never have to face it alone.

No matter how much we grow, no matter where life takes us, you will always be my sisters.

Always yours ❤️
